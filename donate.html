Transparency & Trust

Safe Giving, Clearly Explained

How the donation flow works today, how real payments will be activated, and answers to common questions.

When the foundation is ready, donations will be processed through a licensed Indian payment gateway such as Razorpay, using a secure backend. In brief: the website asks the foundation's server to create a payment order; the gateway's hosted checkout (which is PCI-DSS compliant) collects payment details; the server verifies the gateway's cryptographic signature before confirming the donation; and webhooks reconcile any payments that confirm asynchronously.

Donors' card details and UPI credentials are never entered on — or stored by — this website. The step-by-step activation guide for administrators is documented in the project README under "Activating real payments".

Contributions support the foundation's seven areas of service — elderly care, child welfare, children's education, temple rejuvenation and cultural activities, medical camps, village welfare, and gaushala/cow welfare — along with the modest administrative costs of carrying out this work responsibly.

The foundation supports existing institutions rather than operating its own, so contributions are directed as supplies, materials, and activity support in coordination with those institutions. As the foundation begins reporting on its activities, a record of how contributions were used will be shared with donors.

A contribution acknowledgement will be sent to the email address provided once the donation system is active. Details of any applicable tax benefits will be published here only after the foundation's relevant registrations have been verified — no tax-exemption claims are made on this website at present.

Important: No bank account number, UPI ID, or QR code is shown on this page. This is intentional — until the foundation activates a verified, secure payment backend, displaying such details would put donors at risk. No registration numbers or tax-exemption claims are displayed either, for the same reason.
